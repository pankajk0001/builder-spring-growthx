# Role-board image layout

This specification records structure only; the local reference and its real club details are not published.

- Portrait board, rendered at twice logical size for WhatsApp readability.
- Blue headers (#064763), red rows (#982438), white text and fine pale dividers.
- Bundled Inter, with bold role labels and section headings; center aligned text.
- Two columns: roles on the left; assigned names or Open on the right.
- Top header: fictional club badge, club name, meeting number, date and time.
- Opening rows: TMOD, Word & Idiom Master, Movie-Master.
- Prepared Speakers section: Speaker 1 paired with Evaluator 1; likewise 2 and 3. Each evaluator stays in the matching speaker row.
- Second Section: Table Topic Master.
- Third Section: General Evaluator, Timer, Grammarian, Ah Counter, Listener.
- Closing line: ALL THE BEST EVERYONE.
- Subtle drawn botanical linework behind red rows; every name remains readable.
- Rows expand for long names, keeping all text within its own cell. No role or holder may be omitted silently.
- Test preview goes to Secretary self-chat only, with the exact source table in its caption.
